# 03 · FitBud Terms for Clients (Trainees)

> **DRAFT for counsel review — not for publication.** Prepared 2 Nov 2026.
> Written to be read by an ordinary person, in plain language, because the people who sign it are consumers giving us health information. Please keep it readable when you mark it up.

**Effective date:** [[DATE]] · **Version:** 1.0-draft

These terms add to the FitBud **Terms of Service** and apply when you use FitBud as someone's client. Where they conflict, these win.

You accept them when you accept your trainer's invitation.

---

## 1. What FitBud does for you

**1.1** Your trainer uses FitBud to tell you what to do — workouts, meals, check-ins — and you use it to record what you actually did. Your trainer can then see how the week really went instead of asking you.

**1.2 Your trainer coaches you. FitBud does not.** Every plan, target, instruction and piece of advice you see in FitBud was written by your trainer. We do not write any of it, we do not check it, and we do not give you fitness, nutrition, medical or health advice of our own. If you have a question about your plan, ask your trainer.

**1.3 We did not choose your trainer and we do not vouch for them.** You and your trainer arranged to work together outside FitBud. We do not check anyone's qualifications, certifications, insurance or experience, and we do not supervise or approve what they tell you to do.

**1.4** FitBud is not a medical device and does not monitor your health.

## 2. Who can use it

You must be **18 or over**. FitBud is not for anyone younger, in any role. If you are under 18, do not accept the invitation; tell your trainer.

## 3. Your account

**3.1** You join by accepting an invitation from your trainer. You cannot register on your own, and you can have one trainer at a time.

**3.2** The account is yours. Use it yourself. Do not let your trainer — or anyone else — sign in as you or log things on your behalf as if they were you.

**3.3** Keep your password to yourself. Tell us at tech@thefitbud.com if you think someone else has got into your account.

**3.4** During the beta, your trainer looks after the email address and mobile number you sign in with. If either changes, ask your trainer to update it.

## 4. What you give us, and what your trainer can see

**4.1 What we ask of you.** FitBud is built so that the normal day takes very little input: confirming you ate what was planned, marking a set done, filling a check-in that is pre-filled from last time. Detail is asked for only where the coaching needs it.

**4.2 Your trainer sees your coaching.** For as long as [[your trainer]] is coaching you, they can see everything you record about it:

- the workouts and meals you logged, when you logged them, and when you logged one late;
- your answers to check-ins, including anything you write in your own words;
- your body measurements, and any progress photographs you upload;
- your date of birth, gender and the units you prefer;
- the information you gave when you joined, in the form your trainer put together;
- your meal times, your reminder settings and your quiet hours;
- whether you have been responding to reminders;
- notes your trainer writes about you — some of which they may choose to show you, and some of which they keep to themselves.

**4.3 Nobody else sees it.** No other trainer on FitBud can see anything about you. We do not sell your information, we do not advertise to you, we do not use it to train any model, and we do not give it to anyone except the service providers listed in the Privacy Policy, who process it for us so that FitBud works.

**4.4 Health information.** Some of what your trainer asks you for will be about your body and your health. You decide what to share. If you are not comfortable giving something, say so to your trainer — but understand that they may not be able to coach you safely without it.

**4.5 Photographs.** Uploading a progress photograph is your choice and never required by FitBud. Your trainer can see any photograph you upload. Your trainer is not allowed to download, publish, post or show it to anyone else without your specific permission, and permission to coach you is not permission to publish your photograph. If a trainer does that, tell us at tech@thefitbud.com.

**4.6 Be honest, for your own sake.** The point of logging is that your trainer sees what really happened. Nothing in FitBud scores or grades you, there are no percentages and no streaks, and a missed session is recorded as missed, not as a failure. A late log is marked logged late, never quietly closed.

## 5. What we ask of you

**5.1** Give accurate information, especially about your health. Your trainer relies on it to keep you safe.

**5.2** Tell your trainer promptly if your health changes in a way that affects your training — an injury, an illness, a pregnancy, a new medication, a new diagnosis.

**5.3** Use FitBud only for your own coaching, and follow the **Acceptable Use Policy**.

## 6. Your health and safety

**6.1** The **Health and Fitness Disclaimer** forms part of these terms. Please read it.

**6.2** Exercise and changing what you eat carry real risk of injury and of harm to your health. **Get clearance from a doctor before you start**, particularly if you have any heart, lung, joint, metabolic or mental-health condition, if you are pregnant or recently gave birth, if you are recovering from surgery or an injury, or if you have been inactive for a long time.

**6.3** **Stop immediately and get medical help** if you feel chest pain, severe breathlessness, dizziness, faintness, sudden or severe pain, or anything else that frightens you. Do not finish a set, a session or a plan because FitBud is showing it to you.

**6.4** You decide whether to do what your plan says. Nothing in FitBud obliges you to complete an exercise that hurts or feels unsafe. Tell your trainer.

**6.5** Reminders are a convenience. Do not rely on FitBud to remind you about medication or anything medically important.

## 7. Reminders and messages

**7.1** FitBud can send you push notifications about things you have not logged yet. You can turn them off, set quiet hours, and change your meal times, in the app's settings.

**7.2** We will not spam you. There are no promotional notifications, and a reminder is sent only when the item is still unlogged.

**7.3 WhatsApp.** If you choose to ask your trainer something through FitBud, it opens WhatsApp with a message prepared for you and **you** press Send. We do not send it for you and we never read or store your WhatsApp conversations — FitBud only records that you asked about a particular item, so your trainer knows to answer. Separately, if you opt in, FitBud may itself send you reminders on WhatsApp; that is your choice, you are asked for it explicitly, and you can withdraw it.

## 8. Money

**8.1** You pay your trainer directly, outside FitBud, on whatever terms the two of you agreed. **We never take any payment from you.**

**8.2** Your trainer may record in FitBud what you owe and whether you have paid. That is their bookkeeping. We do not verify it, we do not collect it, and we are not involved in it. If you disagree with something your trainer has recorded, take it up with them.

**8.3** FitBud itself is free to you, and we have no plans to charge clients.

## 9. If coaching ends

**9.1** Either you or your trainer can end the coaching relationship. Ending it in FitBud does not end any agreement you have with your trainer outside it.

**9.2** After it ends, the record becomes read-only. Your trainer keeps a read-only view of the coaching they delivered; they cannot change it or add to it.

**9.3 You keep your history.** Your logs, check-ins, measurements and photographs stay yours and stay visible to you in the app.

**9.4** If you later go back to the **same** trainer, everything comes back as it was. If you start with a **different** trainer, they begin with a completely blank workspace — they get nothing from your previous coaching, and nothing you shared with your previous trainer is passed on.

## 10. Your choices about your data

You can, at any time:

- **see** what we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- **withdraw** a consent you gave, including the WhatsApp opt-in;
- **ask us to delete** your account and your data — during the beta, by emailing **tech@thefitbud.com**; we are building this into the app;
- **complain** to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

The Privacy Policy explains all of this, including how long we keep things and what happens to your coaching record when you ask us to delete your account.

## 11. Ending your use

You can stop using FitBud at any time, and ask us to delete your account as §10 describes. You do not need your trainer's permission.

### Document notes (delete before publication)

- §4.2 is written from the actual data model, so that "what your coach can see" in the app (TR-20) and this clause say the same thing. The list must be checked against the schema whenever a new trainer-visible field is added.
- §4.2 includes trainer notes because of D20: a note marked "Visible to client" is shown to the trainee; an unmarked note stays private to the trainer. The clause is deliberately honest that private notes exist.
- §4.6 reflects the product rules: no percentages, no streaks, no auto-resolve, and "logged late" rather than auto-closed.
- §7.3 reflects D2 (an "asked coach" marker is recorded; message content never is) and D17/D11 (FitBud-sent WhatsApp requires a recorded explicit opt-in).
- §9.4 reflects D21.
- Steps are not mentioned anywhere in this draft, because step collection is not active (`steps_enabled` is false). When it is switched on, §4.2 must gain a line — steps are synced and trainer-visible under D35 as superseded on 30 Oct 2026 — and the Privacy Policy section on steps must be published at the same time.
