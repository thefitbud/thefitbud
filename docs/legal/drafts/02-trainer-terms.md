# 02 · FitBud Trainer Terms

> **DRAFT for counsel review — not for publication.** Prepared 2 Nov 2026.

**Effective date:** [[DATE]] · **Version:** 1.0-draft

These Trainer Terms add to the FitBud **Terms of Service** and apply to you when you use FitBud as a trainer. Where they conflict with the Terms of Service, these Trainer Terms win. Terms defined there have the same meaning here.

You accept these Trainer Terms when you register a trainer account.

---

## 1. You are an independent professional

**1.1** You coach your clients independently. You are not our employee, agent, partner, franchisee or representative, and nothing in this agreement creates any of those relationships. You have no authority to bind us or to speak for us.

**1.2** We do not supervise, direct, review or approve your coaching. We set no standards for it, and we do not check that you meet any.

**1.3** You are responsible for your own tax, registration, licensing, professional-body membership and insurance obligations.

**1.4** You decide your own prices, terms, cancellation policy and refund policy with your clients. Those arrangements are between you and your client, and we are not a party to them.

## 2. What you warrant to us

By using FitBud as a trainer you confirm, each time you use it, that:

**2.1** You are 18 or over and legally able to enter this agreement.

**2.2** You are competent to provide the coaching you provide, and you hold any qualification, certification, registration, licence or insurance that the law or your profession requires for it. We do not verify this; we rely on your word.

**2.3** You will coach within the limits of your competence, and you will refer a client to a qualified medical or other professional where their needs go beyond those limits.

**2.4** Every client you invite is 18 or over, and has genuinely agreed to be coached by you and to use FitBud for it.

**2.5** The information you give us about a client in order to invite them — their name, email address and mobile number — is accurate, and you obtained it from that client for this purpose.

**2.6** Everything you put into FitBud is yours to put there and infringes nobody's rights.

> **COUNSEL:** the owner has decided not to require any qualification declaration during the beta and not to verify trainers. §2.2 is therefore the only mechanism allocating coaching competence to the trainer. Please confirm it is worded strongly enough to be relied on, and whether a one-line affirmative tick at registration ("I confirm I hold the qualifications and insurance my coaching requires") should be captured in the product. Decided 3 Nov 2026: captured as its own tick box at trainer registration (draft 12 §2.1). No qualification is checked.

## 3. Your coaching is yours

**3.1** You decide what to ask your clients, what to prescribe them, how to coach them, and how to manage the relationship. We provide the tool; the professional judgment is entirely yours.

**3.2** Every plan, target, instruction, note and coaching decision in your clients' accounts is **your** work, given by you, and **your** responsibility. FitBud neither authors nor reviews any of it. FitBud makes no coaching decision and gives no coaching advice, to you or to your clients, and nothing the software displays is a recommendation by us.

**3.3** FitBud shows you where a client's recorded behaviour diverged from what you expected, and the reason it derived that from. It does not tell you what to do about it, and it never resolves anything for you. What you do with that information is your decision.

**3.4** You are responsible for the safety of what you prescribe, including screening a client for conditions that make an exercise or a dietary change unsafe, and for telling a client to seek medical advice where they should.

**3.5** You will not present FitBud as providing, endorsing or validating your coaching, and you will not tell a client that FitBud has checked, approved or certified you or your plans.

## 4. Your clients' personal data

This section is the heart of this agreement. Read it.

**4.1 Our role and yours.** FitBud is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data in the platform under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You access your clients' personal data as an **authorised user** of FitBud, under this agreement. You are separately and fully responsible for any personal data you take out of FitBud and hold yourself.

> **COUNSEL:** this allocation is the subject of question Q2 in the brief (`00-counsel-brief.md` §4). If you conclude that trainers are independent fiduciaries for the forms they author, this clause and the Privacy Policy both change, and a data-sharing annex must be added here.

**4.2 Purpose limitation.** You may use a client's personal data **only** to coach that client. You must not use it for marketing, for research, to train any model, to build a mailing list, to sell or share with anyone, or for any purpose that client would not expect from the coaching they asked for.

**4.3 Confidentiality.** Treat everything you learn about a client through FitBud as confidential: their health information, body measurements, photographs, check-in answers, eating and training behaviour, and what they tell you. Do not disclose any of it to anyone without that client's explicit, specific permission, unless the law requires you to.

**4.4 What you may ask for.** You can compose your own onboarding and check-in questions. When you do:

- (a) ask only for what you genuinely need to coach that client safely and effectively;
- (b) **never** ask for an identity number (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence, voter ID or any other), a bank account number, a card number, a UPI ID or any other financial credential;
- (c) **never** ask for personal data about anyone who is not your client;
- (d) do not ask for a diagnosis, a medical record or a test result unless it is genuinely necessary for the coaching, and tell the client why you are asking;
- (e) do not use a free-text field to work around (a) to (d).

We may remove a question or a form that breaks this clause, and may suspend your account for it.

**4.5 Photographs.** A client's photographs — progress, meal and check-in photos alike — are sensitive. Require a photo only where it genuinely helps you coach that client, and if a client tells you they would rather not send photos, respect that and switch the requirement off. You may look at a client's photographs to coach that client. You must not download, copy, forward, publish, post or show them to anyone — including in marketing, on social media, in a testimonial or in a case study — unless that client has given you specific written permission for that specific use. Permission to be coached is not permission to publish.

**4.6 Data you keep yourself.** If you copy a client's information into your own notes, spreadsheets, phone or WhatsApp, that copy is yours and you are responsible for it: for keeping it secure, for using it only for coaching, for deleting it when it is no longer needed, and for answering that client if they ask you about it. Our deletion of data from FitBud does not delete your copies, and we cannot do it for you.

**4.7 When coaching ends.** When a relationship ends, your access becomes read-only, limited to the history of the coaching you delivered. You must stop using that client's data for anything other than keeping your own records and meeting your own legal obligations. If the client later coaches with someone else, that trainer gets nothing from your relationship, and you get nothing from theirs.

**4.8 Requests from clients.** If a client asks you to give them their data, correct it or delete it, send them to tech@thefitbud.com and tell us at the same address. Do not try to handle a data-protection request yourself, and do not refuse one. If a client withdraws consent, their account changes accordingly and you must stop using any copy you hold.

**4.9 If something goes wrong.** Tell us at tech@thefitbud.com **within 24 hours** if you learn or suspect that your account has been accessed by someone else, that client data has gone somewhere it should not, or that you have lost a device with client data on it. We may have a legal duty to report it, and we cannot do so if we do not know.

**4.10 Help us when we have to answer.** If we receive a data-protection request, a complaint or a regulator's query that concerns your clients, you will give us the information we reasonably need to answer it.

## 5. Messages you send

**5.1** FitBud never sends a message as you. Where it offers WhatsApp, it opens WhatsApp on your device with text prepared for you and **you** press Send. Whatever you then send is yours: its content, its timing and its consequences, including under Meta's policies and any law on unsolicited messages.

**5.2** Do not use FitBud's pre-filled messages to market to people who have not asked to hear from you, to contact someone who has told you to stop, or to send anything the Acceptable Use Policy prohibits.

**5.3** If and when FitBud itself sends messages to your clients — reminders and service notifications — those are sent by FitBud, identified as FitBud, never as you, and only to clients who have opted in where opt-in is required.

## 6. Payment records

**6.1** You can record what a client has agreed to pay you, how often, when it starts, when it renews, whether it has been paid, and whether it was cash, UPI or a bank transfer. This is a record for you.

**6.2** We do not collect, hold or transmit any money, and we are not a party to your payment arrangement. We issue no invoice, receipt or tax document, and we do not verify that anything recorded as paid was in fact paid. Chasing payment, refunding it and accounting for it are entirely yours.

**6.3** Disputes about money between you and a client are between you and that client.

## 7. Invitations and your clients' accounts

**7.1** You invite a client by giving us their name, email and mobile number. Only invite someone who has agreed to it.

**7.2** Your client's account is **theirs**, not yours. You must not sign in to it, ask for their password, or record their logs or answers for them as if you were them.

**7.3** During the beta you maintain a client's sign-in email and mobile number on their behalf. Change them only when the client asks you to.

**7.4** You may end a coaching relationship in FitBud. Doing so does not end any contract you have with that client outside FitBud.

## 8. Indemnity

You will indemnify us against any claim, loss, liability, cost or expense (including reasonable legal costs) arising from: your coaching or anything you prescribe; any injury, illness or loss suffered by a client of yours as a result of your coaching; your breach of §4; any claim by a client about their personal data where the cause was your act or omission; or your breach of this agreement.

> **COUNSEL:** see draft 13 P9. Aligned with Terms of Service §13, which limits the indemnity to risks the trainer controls. We have included this knowing it may be of limited practical value against an individual, because it allocates responsibility clearly even if recovery is unlikely.

## 9. Suspension

As well as the grounds in the Terms of Service, we may suspend or close your trainer account immediately if we reasonably believe you have breached §4 (your clients' personal data), §2.4 (inviting a minor), or §7.2 (using a client's account), or that a client is at risk. Where we do, we will tell you why.

## 10. Term

This agreement runs for as long as you hold a trainer account. Clauses 4.3, 4.5, 4.6, 6.3, 8 and anything that by its nature should continue survive its end.

### Document notes (delete before publication)

- §4.4 exists because onboarding and check-in forms are trainer-configurable in the product (`intake_definitions.schema_json`, `checkin_form_templates`, `onboarding_form_templates`). A server-side restriction on field types would make §4.4(b) enforceable rather than merely contractual; recommended as a product task.
- §3.3 reflects the no-auto-resolve product rule and the "explain, don't decide" principle (P14): FitBud states the deterministic reason for an exception and never recommends a coaching action.
- §5.1 reflects D31 (FitBud never auto-sends on a person's behalf) and §5.3 reflects D17 (FitBud-sent WhatsApp utility messages are built but switched off, and are always signed as FitBud).
- §4.7 reflects D21 (reactivation restores history to the same trainer; a different trainer starts blank).
- §7.3 reflects D18 and TR-20: a trainee's sign-in email and mobile are read-only to the trainee, with "Ask your coach to update these".
