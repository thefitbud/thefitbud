## 1. What "closed beta" means here

**1.1** FitBud is distributed to a limited group — approximately 20 trainers and the clients they invite — through **Apple TestFlight** and **Google Play closed testing**. It is not publicly available.

**1.2** You are here because you asked to take part and we accepted, or because a trainer in the beta invited you as their client. Taking part is voluntary: you are not engaged, employed or retained by us, we are not paying you, and you owe us nothing beyond these terms. Do not share your build, your invitation or your access with anyone else, and do not publish screenshots or recordings of unreleased features without asking us first.

**1.3** The software is **pre-release**. It will have bugs. Some features are incomplete, some are switched off, and some will change or be removed.

## 1A. Who operates the beta

FitBud is operated during the beta by **M/s Oryvate**, a partnership firm, as identified in the Terms of Service §1. The partners intend to incorporate a company to carry on the business; if they do, the Terms of Service §18.3 transfers these agreements to it and we will tell you by email. Nothing about your use of FitBud changes because of it.

## 2. Free of charge

FitBud charges you nothing during the beta — not trainers, not clients. We may introduce fees for trainers afterwards, on notice, as the Terms of Service §15 describes. Your use of the beta creates no entitlement to free use later and no obligation to pay for anything.

## 3. What we do not promise

**3.1** To the fullest extent the law allows, the beta is provided **"as is" and "as available"**, with no warranty of any kind: not of fitness for purpose, not of accuracy, not of availability, not of uninterrupted or error-free operation, and not of data preservation.

**3.2** Expect, specifically, that during the beta:

- the app may be unavailable, sometimes without notice;
- reminders and notifications may be late, duplicated or missed;
- data may be displayed incorrectly;
- a feature may change or disappear between builds;
- you may need to update the app to keep using it.

**3.3 Keep your own records of anything important.** We take backups and have rehearsed restoring from them, but we cannot promise that no data will ever be lost during the beta, and you should not make FitBud the only place a coaching record exists.

## 4. Your data in the beta

**4.1** The Privacy Policy applies in full, and nothing in these Beta Terms reduces it. "Beta" is not a reason for us to treat your personal data less carefully.

**4.2 Account deletion is manual during the beta.** In-app deletion is not built yet. **Email tech@thefitbud.com** and we will delete your account and data and confirm when it is done. See the Privacy Policy §12.

**4.3 We will not wipe your data to clear the decks.** If we ever need to reset data as part of ending the beta or a major change, we will tell you by email first and tell you how to get a copy of yours.

**4.4** Features that are built but switched off are listed in the Privacy Policy §5. We will not switch any of them on without telling you and, where consent is needed, asking for it.

## 5. Feedback

**5.1** Please tell us what is wrong: tech@thefitbud.com.

**5.2** When you send us feedback — a bug report, a suggestion, a complaint about how something works — you let us use it to improve FitBud, without any obligation to you and without any payment. You keep whatever rights you have in it; you are simply not charging us for the idea.

**5.3** Do not send us anything confidential, anything belonging to someone else, or anything you want paid for. Do not include a client's personal data in a bug report — give us the screen and what happened, and we will find the record ourselves.

**5.4** We will not use your name, your business name or your logo in any public material about FitBud without asking you first.

> **COUNSEL:** §5.2 is drafted as a licence to use feedback, deliberately not as an assignment of intellectual property. Please confirm it is sufficient and fair for a beta with unpaid participants.

## 6. Support during the beta

Support is by email to tech@thefitbud.com. We aim to reply within one working day. During the beta you may also hear from us directly to ask how something is working; you can tell us to stop.

## 7. Ending the beta

**7.1** We may end the beta, change who is in it, or remove your access, at any time, with reasonable notice where we can give it.

**7.2** When the beta ends, we will tell you what happens next: whether FitBud continues, whether your account carries over, whether fees will apply, and how to get a copy of your data if it will not carry over.

**7.3** You may leave the beta whenever you like. Stop using the app, and email us if you want your account deleted.

## 8. Store rules

The beta reaches you through Apple TestFlight or Google Play closed testing, and their own terms apply to the distribution. The terms that Apple requires us to include, and which apply to Google Play too, are in the Terms of Service §19.
