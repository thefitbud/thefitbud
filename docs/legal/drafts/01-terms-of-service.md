## 5. The coaching relationship and what each party sees

**5.1** When a client accepts a trainer's invitation, a coaching relationship is created between them. For as long as it is active, **the trainer can see everything the client records in FitBud about that coaching** — plans and their execution, meal and workout logs, check-in answers, body measurements, progress photographs and notes.

**5.2** No other trainer can see any of it. A trainer sees only their own clients.

**5.3** When a coaching relationship ends, the record of it becomes **read-only** for both parties. The trainer keeps read access to the history of the coaching they delivered; neither party can change it.

**5.4** If the **same** trainer and client later resume, the relationship is reactivated and its history, configuration and the trainer's notes return as they were. If the client starts with a **different** trainer, that trainer begins with an empty workspace and receives nothing from the previous relationship.

**5.5** The client keeps access to their own coaching history in the app regardless of which relationships have ended.
