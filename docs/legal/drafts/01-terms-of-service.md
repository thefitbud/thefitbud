## 2. What FitBud is, and what it is not

**2.1 What it is.** FitBud is software that helps an independent personal trainer and their client work together. A trainer records what they expect of a client — workouts, meals, check-ins — and the client records what they actually did. FitBud shows the trainer where the two diverged.

We do not give fitness, nutritional, medical or health advice, and we make no coaching decisions. Everything of that kind inside FitBud was written by a human being — your trainer — and is their work and their responsibility, not ours. See also the **Health and Fitness Disclaimer**, which forms part of these Terms.

**2.3 FitBud does not introduce trainers to clients.** There is no directory, search, ranking, matching or recommendation of trainers. You and your trainer find each other and agree your arrangement entirely outside FitBud. We do not vet, verify, certify, endorse, employ or supervise any trainer, and we do not check anyone's qualifications, certifications, registrations or insurance.

**2.4 FitBud does not handle money.** A trainer can record what a client has agreed to pay them and whether it has been paid. That is bookkeeping for the trainer's own reference. **2.5 FitBud is not a messaging service.** There is no chat, voice or video inside FitBud. Where FitBud offers to send something by WhatsApp, it opens WhatsApp on your own device with a message prepared for you, and **you** choose whether to send it. ## 5. The coaching relationship and what each party sees

**5.1** When a client accepts a trainer's invitation, a coaching relationship is created between them. For as long as it is active, **the trainer can see everything the client records in FitBud about that coaching** — plans and their execution, meal and workout logs, check-in answers, body measurements, progress photographs and notes.

**5.2** No other trainer can see any of it. A trainer sees only their own clients.

**5.3** When a coaching relationship ends, the record of it becomes **read-only** for both parties. The trainer keeps read access to the history of the coaching they delivered; neither party can change it.

**5.4** If the **same** trainer and client later resume, the relationship is reactivated and its history, configuration and the trainer's notes return as they were. If the client starts with a **different** trainer, that trainer begins with an empty workspace and receives nothing from the previous relationship.

**5.5** The client keeps access to their own coaching history in the app regardless of which relationships have ended.
