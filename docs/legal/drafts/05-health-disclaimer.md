# 05 · Health and Fitness Disclaimer

> **DRAFT for counsel review — not for publication.** Prepared 2 Nov 2026.
> This is the document that matters most if somebody gets hurt. It is deliberately short, plain and prominent. Please do not let a markup make it longer than a person will read.

**Effective date:** [[DATE]] · **Version:** 1.0-draft

This disclaimer forms part of the FitBud Terms of Service. It must be shown to every client before they accept an invitation, and be available in the app at any time.

---

## 1. FitBud does not give you health or fitness advice

FitBud is software. It does not create plans, set targets, prescribe exercises or meals, or give fitness, nutrition, dietary, medical or health advice of any kind.

**Everything of that sort in FitBud comes from your trainer**, a person you chose, who is solely responsible for it. We do not write it, review it, approve it or check that it is safe or suitable for you.

## 2. FitBud is not medical care

FitBud is **not** a medical device, a diagnostic tool or a monitoring system. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent or monitor any disease, injury or condition, and it does not watch over your health. Nothing in FitBud is a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor or other healthcare professional.

**Never ignore medical advice, or delay getting it, because of something you saw in FitBud.**

## 3. We do not check your trainer

We do not verify, certify, approve or endorse any trainer. We do not check anyone's qualifications, certifications, registrations, insurance or experience, and we do not supervise or assess their coaching. You chose your trainer yourself, outside FitBud, and your coaching arrangement is with them.

## 4. Get medical clearance before you start

Exercise and changes to what you eat carry a real risk of injury, and in some circumstances of serious harm. **Consult a qualified medical practitioner before beginning or substantially changing any exercise or nutrition programme**, and tell them what you intend to do.

This matters especially if you:

- have, or have ever had, a heart condition, high blood pressure, chest pain, or an irregular heartbeat;
- have a lung or breathing condition, including asthma;
- have diabetes or another metabolic condition;
- have a joint, bone, back, muscle or tendon problem, or are recovering from an injury or an operation;
- have a neurological condition, or ever lose balance or consciousness;
- have an eating disorder, or a history of one;
- have any mental-health condition that your training or your eating could affect;
- are pregnant, have recently given birth, or are breastfeeding;
- take any medication that affects your heart rate, blood pressure, blood sugar, balance or hydration;
- are over [[AGE]], or have been inactive for a long time;
- have any condition that makes exercise or a change of diet risky for you.

If any of these apply, do not rely on your trainer's judgment alone, however experienced they are. Get medical clearance.

## 5. Stop if something is wrong

**Stop exercising at once, and get medical help**, if you experience chest pain or pressure, severe or unusual breathlessness, dizziness, light-headedness, faintness, nausea, blurred vision, sudden or severe pain, numbness, a joint giving way, or anything that frightens you.

Do not finish the set, the session or the day's plan because FitBud is showing it to you. **Nothing in FitBud obliges you to do anything.** The app shows what your trainer expects; it does not decide what your body can do. If an exercise hurts or feels unsafe, do not do it, and tell your trainer.

## 6. Tell your trainer what is going on

Your trainer can only coach you safely if they know your situation. Tell them, honestly and promptly, about:

- any medical condition, injury, surgery, allergy or medication relevant to your training or your diet;
- any change in your health — a new injury, an illness, a pregnancy, a new diagnosis, a new medication;
- any pain, discomfort or symptom you notice while training or after it.

If you withhold or misstate this, no plan built on it can be safe.

## 7. Nutrition information is approximate

Food and nutrition information in FitBud comes from public databases and from entries created by trainers and clients. Energy and macronutrient figures are **estimates** and may be wrong for the food in front of you. They are not a clinical or dietetic assessment. FitBud does not detect allergens and must not be relied on for an allergy, intolerance, or any medically prescribed diet. If you have a food allergy, a medical dietary requirement or a condition affected by what you eat, work with a qualified dietitian or your doctor, and check every food yourself.

## 8. Exercise descriptions and illustrations

Exercise illustrations and descriptions in FitBud come partly from third-party libraries, licensed and credited on our Credits and Licences page. They are general reference material. They are **not** instruction tailored to you, and following a picture is not the same as being taught a movement. Technique that is wrong for your body can injure you. If you are not confident in an exercise, ask your trainer to teach it to you in person.

## 9. Reminders are not a safety system

FitBud's reminders exist to help you log what you did. They depend on your device, your operating system, your network and third-party delivery services, and any of them may fail. **Do not rely on FitBud to remind you about medication or anything else of medical importance.**

## 10. You accept the risk

By using FitBud to follow a coaching plan you acknowledge that:

- exercise and dietary change carry an inherent risk of injury and of harm to health, which cannot be eliminated;
- you take part voluntarily and you accept that risk;
- you are responsible for exercising within your own limits and for stopping when you should;
- your trainer, not FitBud, is responsible for the coaching they give you;
- to the fullest extent the law allows, FitBud is not liable for any injury, illness, health consequence or loss arising from exercise, training or dietary change, or from any plan or instruction your trainer gives you.

Nothing in this disclaimer limits any liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death or personal injury caused by our own negligence.

## 11. In an emergency

**FitBud is not an emergency service and nobody at FitBud is monitoring your health.** In a medical emergency in India, call **112**, or go to the nearest hospital. Do not use FitBud, and do not message your trainer, to get help in an emergency.
