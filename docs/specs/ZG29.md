## Outcome
Users can delete their account from Privacy in the trainee app and trainer web; it is archived for 6 months, and signing in again during that time offers to reactivate it. The screens say how to get it deleted permanently straight away (email tech@thefitbud.com).

## Context
- Covers master-list (v13) items BP1. Everything the implementer needs from those rows is pasted here.
- Capture on Expo web at 393x852 (iOS frames) or 412x915 (Android frames); QA1's nightly Maestro run re-captures on Android after merge.
- First read the P1 audit for this bucket in `docs/qa/P1/` (ZA2, W5); fix only the differences it lists for these frames, and add any it missed to the PR description.
- Uses ZB14's API (W6): `POST /account/deletion` (fresh OTP or password proof), `GET /account/status`, `POST /account/reactivation`; sign-in returns `accountState: "archived"` with `purgeAfter` for an archived account. Rules: D-W6-7, D-W6-8 in `docs/specs/batches/W6.md`; copy: D-W11-6 in `docs/specs/batches/W11.md`.
- App Store and Play both require in-app deletion.
- All copy lives in `packages/core/src/account-deletion-copy.ts` and is used by both apps (D-W11-6). Dates read like "Tue 4 May 2027" (`formatCivilDate`), never ISO.
- **Delete flow** (Privacy → Delete account): explainer, typed confirm `DELETE`, fresh OTP or password proof, then the request; on success the app signs out and shows the archived notice. A trainer with active clients gets ZB14's `409 active_clients` and the "end coaching first" copy with a link to Clients, and nothing is archived.
- **Reactivate flow:** after sign-in, an archived account goes to the reactivate screen instead of the app (the two `AuthProvider.tsx` files only route an archived account to the new screen; the screens live in the new files). "Reactivate my account" calls `POST /account/reactivation` and continues into the app; "Sign out" signs out. Coaching that ended with the deletion stays ended (D-W6-8), and the screen says so.
- Planned frame (not in the repo yet; FitBud UI draws it, Firoz reviews it): `BP1-delete-account-ios.png` (393x852), `BP1-delete-account-web.png` (1440x900), `BP1-reactivate-account-ios.png` (393x852) and `BP1-reactivate-account-web.png` (1440x900). Until the PNG is in `docs/design/mockups/png/`, the parts that need it stop and report; the rest runs.
