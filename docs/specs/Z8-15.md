## Outcome
From a client's workspace the trainer opens an assigned workout and fills in sets, reps and kg after an in-person session; the workout shows as coach-logged.

## Context
- In-person coaching, added to the beta by Firoz on 4 Nov 2026 (PLAN-v2 D-P2-15). Default rules R1–R3 (recommended, Firoz can override): R1 a trainer-started workout is read-only to the trainee, who sees "Logged by your coach"; R2 trainers are exempt from Z6-11 log-time limits for the log-workout form, backdate cap 7 days; R3 a coach-logged workout counts once toward adherence and streaks, keyed by the workout execution id, never double-counted.
- Written against `main` at c398f67. The implementer re-checks each item against the latest `main` before building (AGENTS.md).
- Route `/clients/:id/log-workout/:assignmentId`, entry button "Log workout" on assigned workouts in the client workspace. Uses Z6-19 `POST /workouts/assignments/:id/log`.
- Prefill from the plan's prescribed sets; Bodyweight toggle; date picker limited to the last 7 days (R2).
- `WorkoutPanel.tsx` shows a "Logged by coach" tag for `loggedByRole=coach`.
- Appended to the W8 settings lane (owns `App.tsx`).
- Design guide for every UI task: `docs/design/README.md`, `docs/design/DESIGN-NOTES.md`, `docs/design/DECISIONS-21OCT.md`. Dates read like "Wed 21 Oct" (never ISO), enums through `labelFor`, touch targets ≥ 44 pt / 44 px, tabular numbers.
- Design-review: until Z11-1 is on `main` (PLAN-v2 D-P2-7) the reviewer compares captures with frames side by side and the PR says "design-review tool not on main yet".
