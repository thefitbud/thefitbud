## Outcome
When a client fixes a meal entry within their 24 hours, the coach can tell: a quiet "Edited" tag on the row, who changed it and when, and what it was before; a removed log shows as its own activity row.

## Context
- D-W14-22 (e) and **D-W14-23** (FitBud UI, 24 Nov 2026). Data from Z14-11 (`lastEditedByRole`, `lastEditedAt`, previous value, removal activity item); no new endpoint.
- Pattern to copy: `WorkoutPanel.tsx:11-20` ("Edited by coach · {day}"). Neutral tag (fill / ink2), never an alert colour, no push, no badge count.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Trainer web Activity and the client day: a meal row whose entry was last edited by the client shows a small neutral **Edited** tag; the meal panel shows "Edited by {client first name} · Sat 14 Nov, 1:50 pm" and "Was: 610 kcal · changed portion" (or the previous outcome).
- [ ] A removal shows as an activity row "Removed the lunch log" with the time; the meal then reads as not logged.
- [ ] Trainer app (`ActivityMealSheet`, activity model): the same tag and line.
- [ ] Coach edits keep today's "Edited by coach" wording; nothing changes for entries the client never edited.
