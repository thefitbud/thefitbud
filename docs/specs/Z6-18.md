## Outcome
Workout, meal and measurement entries record who created and who last edited them, every trainer change has an audit row with before and after, and trainees have a coach-updates notification setting.

## Context
- Trainer log and edit, in the beta per Firoz 4 Nov 2026 (PLAN-v2 **D-P2-21**, replacing the live-logging part of D-P2-15). Live logging by the trainer (one client or several in parallel) is **on hold, Phase 10 (W14 Z14-5, Z14-14, Z14-15)**. Rules (recommended defaults, Firoz can override): **R1** an entry the coach created is read-only to the trainee, labelled "Logged by your coach"; an entry the trainee logged and the coach edited stays the trainee's, labelled "Edited by your coach". **R2** trainers skip the Z6-11 log-time limits, but an entry's date must be within the last 7 days and not in the future; edits are allowed on entries dated within the last 7 days. **R3** a coach-created or coach-edited entry counts once toward adherence, keyed by the entry id, never double-counted. **R4** every trainer create or edit writes one audit record (who, what, when, before and after) and one activity-feed event. **R5** the trainee gets a push for each trainer create or edit only if their "Coach updates to my logs" setting is on (default on; respects push on/off and quiet hours; merged per entry within 10 minutes). Ended clients are read-only (no create or edit).
- In scope (client-side logs that exist on `main` at c677957): **workouts** (`workout_executions` + `set_executions`), **meals** (`meal_compliance`: followed, deviated or skipped, with the deviation note), **body measurements** (`measurements`, incl. weight). **Not in scope:** check-ins (the client's own answers that the trainer reviews; editing them would change what the review is about), steps (no steps log on `main`; Z9-9 reads device totals, which are not editable), progress photos.
- Written against `main` at c677957. The implementer re-checks each item against the latest `main` before building (AGENTS.md).
- On `workout_executions`, `meal_compliance` and `measurements`: `logged_by_role` text NOT NULL default `trainee` (`trainee` | `coach`), `logged_by_user_id` text NULL (FK `users`), `last_edited_by_role` text NULL, `last_edited_by_user_id` text NULL, `last_edited_at` text NULL. Existing rows backfill to `trainee`.
- New table `log_edit_events`: `id`, `coaching_relationship_id` (FK), `trainee_user_id` (FK), `actor_user_id` (FK), `actor_role`, `entity_type` (`workout_execution` | `meal_compliance` | `measurement`), `entity_id`, `action` (`create` | `edit`), `before_json` (NULL on create), `after_json`, `created_at`; index on (`coaching_relationship_id`, `created_at`).
- `notification_preferences.coach_log_updates` integer boolean NOT NULL default 1 (R5). Add `coach_log_updated` to the `notifications.notification_type` enum.
- Next free migration number after Z6-16, in the W6 serial migration lane. Remote application after merge by the batch's agent or FitBud UI (manual list P2).
- Replaces the 4 Nov D-P2-15 version (`log_mode` live/form is dropped: no live logging in the beta).

## Out of scope

- Product rules (CLAUDE.md, never break): only the trainer resolves an exception (no auto-resolve); no auto-send (WhatsApp is a wa.me prefill); no percentages or streaks; payments are tracking only; ended clients are read-only on server and UI.
