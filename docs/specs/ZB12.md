## Outcome
After a client is ended and reactivated, back-logging something dated inside the ended period changes nothing about the exceptions closed at the end.

## Context
- Covers master-list (v13) items EC1d (EC1 rule 1, locked 23 Oct). Everything the implementer needs from those rows is pasted here.
- EC1 rule 1: the ended period stays frozen. `apps/api/src/domain/relationship-lifecycle.ts:102-115` closes open exceptions at end with `closedReason: "coaching_ended"` and `resolvedAt` = end time.
- Rule: a log whose `clientLoggedAt` < the latest `ended_at` of that relationship is rejected 422 `ended_period`; evaluation ignores facts dated before the latest `ended_at` when re-evaluating after reactivation.
- Reactivation flow exists (`/relationships/:id/reactivate`); do not change it.
- Evaluation lives in `apps/api/src/domain/exceptions.ts`; no other W6 lane owns it (ZB10 calls its resolve path without editing it).
