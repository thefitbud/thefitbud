## Outcome
The check-in review page and the Check-ins tab have no layout glitches at the iPad widths, speak in first names only, and show dates and changes with weekday and unit.

## Problem (all from `qa-captures/w13-7/`)
1. **Context card (1024x1366, `W-163-checkin-review-1024x1366.png`):** under "Skipped workout" the "Attention" pill stretches to the full row width, and "Review" is a borderless grey word, not a button. Both should match the Home rows: a content-width Attention pill and a Quiet **Review** button.
2. **"Visible to client" checkbox:** at 1440 and 1180 it is a 12 px native box (below any tap target); at 1024 `tap-targets.css` turns it into an oversized 44 px square (`W-163-checkin-review-1024x1366.png`, `W-163-checkin-review-1180x820.png`). Build it as the design system's checkbox (about 18 px box) inside a label whose hit area is at least 44 px high at every width, and write the label as the frame does: **"{First} can see this note"** (no "client", no pronoun). The helper line above keeps "Private unless you mark it visible to {First}."
3. **Dates and units:** "Submitted 24 Nov, 9:00 am" has no weekday (the app writes "Tue 24 Nov, 9:00 am", as the Check-ins tab list already does); the This week "Change" column shows "+1" and "—" with no unit: show "+1 kg" / "—" (use each measure's unit, like the Before and Latest cells).
4. **Outcome segment:** "Acknowledged · Needs follow-up · Adjust coaching" is tight to the edges at 1180 and 1440 (`W-163-checkin-review-1180x820.png`): let the segment grow (equal columns, 12 px minimum text, no clipping, wrap to two lines if needed) instead of touching the border.
5. **Pronouns:** `ClientCheckinsPage.tsx` still infers his/her/their from `traineeProfile.gender` (`possessivePronoun`, lines ~216, 557-576, 715-721: "Her check-ins are read-only.", "{First} has no check-ins from her coaching.", "Check-ins start once their coaching setup is active."). W13-7's spec forbade it; the guess slipped through. Use the first name ("Rohan's check-ins are read-only", "Rohan has no check-ins from this coaching.", "Check-ins start once coaching setup is active.").
