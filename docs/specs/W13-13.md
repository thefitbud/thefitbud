## Outcome
Trainer web's review panel (Home side panel and the Overview modal) reads and behaves like the trainer app's review sheet: one card, three clearly named actions, one way to move between items, no ⋯ menu. Firoz, 25 Nov 2026 (t523u): *take the app's Z14-23 layout on web.* This replaces D-W13-6 and answers W13.md "Questions for Firoz" #1 (decision D-W13-21).

## Model note
Opus, not Sonnet: `ExceptionDrawer.tsx` is about 1,960 lines with four test files of about 2,700 lines, and the task must keep focus trap, history back, undo windows, idempotency and read-only behaviour intact while restructuring the footer, header and navigation.

## Problem
- Today the footer is "Noted, keep open", "Remind", a ⋯ menu (Add note, Resolve several…, Adjust plan, Message on WhatsApp, Open client, Client settings) and "Resolve…", plus two explanatory caption lines and an "Open client" link (`ExceptionDrawer.tsx` ~1735-1900; captures `qa-captures/w13-11/W-02-home-side-panel-1440x900.png`, `qa-captures/w13-6/W-03x-exception-modal-1440x900.png`). The app dropped all of that in Z14-23.
- The Resolve outcome reads "Talked to client" (`ExceptionDrawer.tsx:83`); the app says "Talked to {first}".
- Pager order disagrees with the Overview card: the card lists Critical first then newest, the modal opens "Overdue meal" as "2 of 5" with "Meal change" as item 1 (`qa-captures/w13-6/W-03x-exception-modal-1440x900.png` against `W-03-client-overview-1440x900.png`); the Home panel opens the same item as "1 of 5" (`qa-captures/w13-11/W-02-home-side-panel-1440x900.png`).
- Resolve several… (`BulkPanel.tsx`) lists up to ten rows that all read "Overdue meal · Open today" with nothing to tell them apart (`qa-captures/w13-11/W-02f-panel-resolve-several-1440x900.png`).

## Keep from W14 and web
- Same server calls, undo windows, idempotency keys, offline disabling, ended-client read-only view, resolve-conflict recovery, Esc, focus trap and history back as today. Only the trainer resolves; no auto-send.
- Web-only, kept: **ArrowLeft / ArrowRight** item navigation and **Resolve several…** (BulkPanel, resolvable types only).
- The "Noted by you at {time} · still open" state line with Undo (RP-02 wording), "Reminder sent" notices, the push-off and rate-limit notices (`nudgeNotices.ts`) (these are results shown after an action, not captions).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Footer actions per state, as RP-01…RP-04: open: **Remind {first}** (only for remindable types, the existing predicate), **Noted, keep open**, **Resolve…** (primary). Noted: state line "Noted by you at {time} · still open" with **Undo**; Remind and Resolve… remain. Resolved: read-only outcome line "Resolved by you at {time} · {outcome}" with **Undo** where undo exists today. Ended client: read-only as today.
- [ ] No explanatory caption lines under the buttons ("Sends … a push notification", "Noted keeps it open…"); status that is a result (Reminder sent, push off, limited) still shows as a notice after the action.
- [ ] **No ⋯ menu** in the panel or modal. **Add note** (the Z14-26 note composer with "Only me" / "{first} too") and **WhatsApp** (wa.me prefill, no template, the trainer sends) are two small text actions under the card. **Resolve several…** is a third small text action shown only when two or more items can be resolved together. "Open client", "Adjust plan" and "Client settings" leave the drawer: the avatar and name open the client page (a visible link, keyboard focusable), where Adjust plan and Settings live.
- [ ] **Resolve…** opens the outcome confirm (inline form or sheet as built): **Talked to {first}** · **Plan adjusted** · **No action needed**, optional private note ("Only you see this"), the lock line, **Resolve item**. Keep Z14-36 behaviour (outcome optional, Resolve never disabled). The label uses the client's first name; only if the name is unavailable use "Talked to the client".
- [ ] **One "1 of n" pager** (‹ ›) is the only item navigation; the passive progress dashes stay; no "Next item" button, no "Back to item" link elsewhere. Order of a client's items is identical in the Home panel, the Overview modal and the Overview "Needs attention" card (Critical first, then newest first), and the pager's "n of m" for the opened item equals its position in that list. Add a test with a shared fixture.
- [ ] **All-done summary**: "{n} resolved · {m} noted, still open" with **Next client: {name}** (only when another client is queued) and **Back** (closes). "Next client" appears nowhere else.
- [ ] Resolve several… rows say what they are: "{type} · {meal or workout} · {Wed 21 Oct}" (the same title the single item shows), so the ten rows are distinguishable.
- [ ] Home side panel (docked, 540 px) and Overview modal (720) both use the new layout; 1180 and 1024 iPad widths: all targets at least 44 px, no clipping, footer never covers content.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, the web e2e pass; banned-strings stay green (no "Mark reviewed", "Nudge", "Reviewed, still open").
