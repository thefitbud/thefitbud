## Outcome
Screen readers hear civil dates like "Wed 21 Oct" and plain-language day states from `labelFor`, never raw ISO dates or raw enum words, on WeeklyConsistency and the native date strip.

## Context
- W6 / W5 leftover (D-W7-13). `WeeklyConsistency` currently sets `accessibilityLabel={`${day.date} ${day.state}`}` (ISO + raw state). `DateNavigator.stateLabel` still returns raw words (`"done"`, `"partial"`, …) even after Z6-22 added `due` → `"to do"`.
- Do not assume Z6-22's "To do" display label fixed every spoken string.
- Use `formatCivilDate` (or the existing civil helpers in `@fitbud/core`) and `labelFor("week_day_state", state)`. Map DateNavigator's local state names onto the week_day_state namespace where they align; do not invent new label namespaces.
- Written against `main` at 893af647.

## Gap list
- `packages/ui-mobile/src/WeeklyConsistency.tsx`: spoken label is ISO date + raw state.
