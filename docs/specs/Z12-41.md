## Outcome
The sync status always tells the truth, queued logs send on their own when the phone can reach the API, nothing is duplicated or lost, and the app opens on cached data with no network.

## Context
- Finding 2 (13 Nov, data off: Sync said it couldn't sync, yet the backend returned 200 on Sync now; likely the phone reached the local API over Wi-Fi and the status came from a stale error or the outbox was not updated after the 200) and 13 Nov #2 cold-start part (ISSUE-MAP #9). **Reproduce first** with device logs and the D1 rows, then fix the root cause.
- Rules (prompt): status only from the outbox's real state (waiting, sending, sent, failed with a reason); the queue sends on reconnect (reachability probe now, NetInfo after Z12-57), on app foreground, every 30 s while not empty, and after any successful API call; "Try sending now" (TR-19a) shows Sending…, then the result; client mutation IDs and server dedupe; conflict when the coach changed the plan (TR-19b); a persisted me + relationship snapshot keyed by userId, cleared on sign-out (like the Z11-8 trainer cache).
- Grading (D-W12-27): this task grades TR-19a, TR-19b and the sync behaviour behind TR-20f; TR-06e, TR-07d, W-20 and TR-14e are graded visually by their screen owners on top of this state. Folded **from Z12-27**: the heading "Couldn't send (0)" with a count in parentheses (`SyncStatusScreen.tsx:186`); 87 (conflict) and 88 (sign out unsent) were never audited.
