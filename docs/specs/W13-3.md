## Outcome
The workout and diet builders fill the window like a workspace: library on the left, the plan canvas in the middle, properties on the right. Each pane scrolls on its own, and no blank area is left under any of them (Firoz's brief: "looks like a shippable product"). On iPad the library pane becomes a Library button.

## Problem
- Plan row W13-3 (`docs/design/v3-web/W13-WEB-PLAN.md`), DS §4 (page shell and scroll model) and D-WEB-1. W13-1 (`9fbc5eea`) and W13-2 (`27fd2fa4`) are merged: tokens, `components/ui/*` primitives and the one-scroller shell exist. This task builds on them; no one-off CSS, no new tokens (ask in the PR).
- Today `BuilderLayout` already has `wide` (≥1280, three panes), `narrow` (1024–1279, centre + properties with a library drawer) and `small` (<1024, read-only) modes. The panes do not own their scroll and leave blank bands under short content.
- The builders are used from two places: the template editor (`PlanTemplateEditorPage`) and a client's plan (`PlanBuilderPage`). Both must look and work the same.
