## Outcome
Activity never tells the trainer that a meal or workout that is still days away is "Not logged yet", and never offers Remind for it.

## Problem
- On Wed 25 Nov the Meals list opens at **Sun 29 Nov** and runs down through Sat 28 and Fri 27: every future meal reads "Not logged yet" (amber) with a **Remind** button (`qa-captures/w13-8/W-158-activity-1440x900.png`, `W-158-activity-1024x1366.png`). Remind sends a push to the client; for a meal four days away that is wrong, and the amber chip says something is missing when nothing is due. Frame W-158 starts at **Today** and lists only past and current days.
- Cause: `activityMealChipLabel` maps `pending` to "Not logged yet" and `canNudgeItem` allows Remind for every `pending` meal and every `assigned` workout, with no check against the item's date (`components/activity/activityUtils.ts`, `ActivityRow.tsx` `showNudge`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Days after today (the client's local today, the same "today" the page already uses for its "Today" label) are not listed as day cards in the feed. If the chosen range contains only future days, show the existing empty state with a sentence that names the range ("Nothing to show for this week yet").
- [ ] Today's own day card is labelled **Today · Wed 25 Nov** (frame W-158); other days keep "Tue 24 Nov".
- [ ] Inside today, a meal or workout whose scheduled time has not arrived reads **Planned** (neutral chip, no amber, no Remind). A meal whose window has started and is not logged keeps "Not logged yet" with Remind ("Due now" tone as the frame draws it if the data has a window start; otherwise keep the current chip). An overdue item keeps "Not logged" and Remind.
- [ ] Remind is offered only for items that are due or overdue; ended clients stay read-only. Existing Remind states (Sent, limited, Reminding…) unchanged.
- [ ] Unchanged: segments (Workouts, Meals, Check-ins), Status and date menus, "Planned N kcal a day", Edited/Removed markers, unread dot, the `?date=&entry=` deep link (a deep link to a future-dated entry still opens it).
- [ ] No ISO dates or enum keys on screen; `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, the web e2e pass.

## Tests to add
- `activityUtils`: future-dated pending meal and assigned workout are not nudgeable and read Planned; due and overdue still nudgeable.
- Day grouping: with a fixed clock, tomorrow's group is absent and today's is labelled "Today · …".
- e2e (`w13-8-activity.spec.ts`): with the demo seed no Remind button sits on a row dated after today. Mutation check: remove the date guard and see a test fail.
