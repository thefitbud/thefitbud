## Outcome
A trainer can delete their account from Settings → Privacy & data on the web; signing in again during the 6-month archive offers to reactivate it.

- Split out of Z12-29 (formerly ZG29) on 4 Nov 2026 because web and mobile never share a spec (PLAN-v2 D-P2-6). Account deletion is beta scope (Firoz, 4 Nov 2026, D-P2-3).
