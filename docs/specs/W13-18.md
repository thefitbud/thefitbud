## Outcome
Two numbers with the same label on one Progress screen never disagree, chart labels read in plain words, and the page names the client, not a pronoun.

## Problem
1. **Workouts done is shown twice with different numbers.** The KPI above the weight chart says "38 of 50 Workouts done" (the selected 12 wk range) while the left rail says "Workouts done 44 of 58" (all time), and the Strength view's headline says "38 of 50 in 12 weeks" (`qa-captures/w13-9/W-160-progress-1440x900.png`, `W-160-strength-exercise-1440x900.png`). The decision was to leave them different (W13 Report W13-9); it reads as a bug. Same for Weight: rail "−3.9 kg" against KPI "−3.2 kg since Fri 11 Sep".
2. **"w/c 31 Aug"** on the weekly bars is jargon. Write "Week of 31 Aug".
3. **Pronouns:** `ClientProgressPage.tsx` (~77-80, 360-368, 392, 523) guesses he/she/their ("See her check-in form", "Her progress is read-only and stays here for her…", "after she logs").

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The rail's values follow the selected range, exactly like the KPI row: same number for the same label at every range (4 wk, 12 wk, 6 mo, All, Custom). The rail row gains a quiet second line naming the scope ("last 12 weeks"; "all time" for All) instead of an unlabelled number. Weight's rail change and the KPI "since {date}" are computed over the same window and agree.
- [ ] Weekly bar labels read "Week of 31 Aug" (and "This week").
- [ ] No `possessivePronoun`; copy uses the first name ("See Rohan's check-in form", "Rohan's progress is read-only and stays here for you to look back on", "after Rohan logs").
- [ ] No percentages, no ISO dates; rail and KPI still read "n of m" style; D-W13-12 holds.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, the web e2e pass.

## Tests to add

- Render with `gender` female, male, unset: identical copy.
- Bars label test ("Week of …", never "w/c").
