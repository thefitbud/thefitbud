## Outcome
A trainee cannot log a meal or workout for a time in the future or before that item's window opened, so exceptions are judged on honest times.

## Context
- Covers master-list (v13) items EC1c (EC1 rule 2, locked 23 Oct). Everything the implementer needs from those rows is pasted here.
- EC1 rule 2 (locked by Firoz 23 Oct): reject `clientLoggedAt` in the future beyond a small clock drift, and before the meal window start.
- Drift: 5 minutes. Window start: the meal assignment's local window start in the trainee's zone; workouts: the assignment's local day start.
- Offline sync: a queued log keeps its original `clientLoggedAt`; it passes if it was valid when made.
