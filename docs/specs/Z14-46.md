## Outcome
The intake looks like frame `TR-03`, whatever the length of the form. Each step holds up to three questions, and no step is empty or almost blank. The header reads "Getting to know you" with "Step n of N" under it, above one progress bar. Questions read as headings. The footer has **Back** (from step 2) and **Continue** (last step: **Submit intake**). A ✕ at the top left lets a stuck trainee leave: **Finish later?** with **Keep going** or **Sign out**. Loading uses the app's standard loading state, not bare text.

## Context
- Firoz, staging build 6, iOS, `vikram.onboarding@demo.fitbud.test`: the steps are "mostly filled with blank space", and the trainee could not leave the flow or sign out.
- **Cause:** `IntakeScreen.tsx` always splits the form into exactly 5 steps (`chunkFields`, `TOTAL_STEPS = 5`) and prints fixed step titles ("About you", "Your routine", "Food habits", "Health", "Goals"). The default global form (migration `0001`) has five text fields (Goals, Relevant history, Preferences, Schedule, Limitations). So each step holds one box, and the step titled "Goals" shows the "Limitations" box. A 3-field form gets two "No questions on this step" steps, and a 20-field form gets four per step. The screen uses the older `M2TopNav` and `Progress5` layout, not v3 `TR-03`, and has no close or sign-out control. Back exists only from step 2.
- **D-W14-45** (steps follow the form), **D-W14-46** (✕ and the sheet, Sign out accepted by Firoz 25 Nov 2026), **D-W14-47** (copy and loading), **D-W14-48** (default form unchanged), **D-W12-12** (system theme), **D-W14-38** (Maestro selector guard).
- `Progress5` and `M2TopNav` live in the shared `components/m2/M2Layout.tsx`. **Do not edit that file**; draw the header and progress bar inside `IntakeScreen.tsx`, or use primitives that already exist. Use the existing `components/BottomSheet.tsx` for the sheet and `useAuth().signOut` for sign out.
- Builds on Z14-45 (same file); start from `main` after Z14-45 merges and keep its submit and error behaviour unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Step grouping (D-W14-45):** a pure helper `groupIntakeSteps(fields)` keeps form order and splits fields into steps of at most 3, with `N = clamp(ceil(count / 3), 1, 5)`. When more than 15 fields exist, it spreads them evenly over 5 steps (sizes differ by at most one). No step is ever empty. With zero fields there is one step showing "Your coach hasn't added any questions yet." and **Submit intake**. The default form (5 fields) gives 2 steps: Goals, Relevant history, Preferences / Schedule, Limitations.
- [ ] **Header (TR-03):** ✕ button at the top left (44 pt target, accessible label "Close"), title "Getting to know you", subtitle "Step n of N", and one progress bar filled to n/N under it. No fixed step titles anywhere.
- [ ] **Questions:** each field label is a heading in the frame's style, with the help text under it. The long-text fields keep their current input and maxLength. Required questions carry no asterisk; the Z14-45 line "Answer this question to continue." shows under a missing one.
- [ ] **Footer:** **Back** (secondary) and **Continue** (primary) side by side as in the frame. On step 1 only **Continue** shows, full width. On the last step the primary label is **Submit intake**. Under the buttons, "Answers saved" appears after a successful draft save (D-W14-47: drafts are on the server, so don't print "on your phone"). Before any save, nothing shows there.
- [ ] **Close sheet (D-W14-46):** ✕ opens a bottom sheet with title "Finish later?", body "Your answers are saved. Sign in again any time to carry on.", primary **Keep going** (closes the sheet) and **Sign out** (signs out and lands on sign-in). Before the sheet opens, the current step's answers are saved as a draft. If that save fails, the sheet body reads "We couldn't save your last answers. Sign out anyway?" with the same two actions. Android hardware back on step 1 opens the same sheet (D-W14-24); on later steps it goes to the previous step.
- [ ] **Loading (D-W14-47):** the first load shows the app's standard loading state (spinner, same as `AppRouter`'s `LoadingView` look) under the header area, not bare "Loading…" text. A load error shows the friendly ErrorBanner with **Try again** and the ✕ (so a trainee whose form will not load can still sign out).
- [ ] Submitted (read-only) state is unchanged apart from the new header; the ✕ still offers the sheet.
- [ ] System light and dark both read correctly (D-W12-12). Text scales with Dynamic Type without clipping the footer.
- [ ] F01 Maestro: replace `".* of 5"` with `"Step 1 of .*"`, keep "Getting to know you", and add `assertVisible: "Close"` (by accessibility label). Every selector the flow uses exists in app source (D-W14-38).
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` pass.
