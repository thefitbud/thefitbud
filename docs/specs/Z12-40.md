## Outcome
Moving between past, today and future days is instant and smooth on Today, Diet and Workout: tap or swipe, cached days show with no spinner, and each tab keeps its scroll position.

## Context
- Finding 6 (not traced on main yet: measure before and after).
- One shared day state for the three tabs; a per-day cache keyed by relationship and civil date, stale-while-revalidate, prefetching ±3 days; TR-06f's skeleton only on a first load with no cache.
- Header reads "Thursday, 12 Nov · past day" or "Monday, 16 Nov · planned" as in the mocks; today's dot is the coral ring.

## Gap list

- No swipe-to-change-day on the tab bodies.
- Scroll offset is not kept per tab per day.
- Written against `main` at `3c227e12b`. Re-check each item on the latest `main` before building; drop what is done and say so in the PR (AGENTS.md).
