## Outcome
Users can delete their account from Privacy in the trainee app and trainer web; it is archived for 6 months, and signing in again during that time offers to reactivate it. The screens say how to get it deleted permanently straight away (email tech@thefitbud.com).

- **Split 4 Nov 2026 (PLAN-v2 D-P2-6):** web and mobile never share a spec, so this task builds the trainee app screens and `packages/core/src/account-deletion-copy.ts`; **Z12-30** builds the trainer-web screens on top. Web bullets below are context only. **Now beta scope** (Firoz, 4 Nov 2026, D-P2-3). The BP1 frames are in PR #153 (approved by Firoz 3 Nov); once merged they are in `docs/design/mockups/png/`.

- Uses Z6-14's API (W6): `POST /account/deletion` (fresh OTP or password proof), `GET /account/status`, `POST /account/reactivation`; sign-in returns `accountState: "archived"` with `purgeAfter` for an archived account. Rules: D-W6-7, D-W6-8 in `docs/specs/batches/W6.md`; copy: D-W12-6 in `docs/specs/batches/W12.md`.
- App Store and Play both require in-app deletion.
- All copy lives in `packages/core/src/account-deletion-copy.ts` and is used by both apps (D-W12-6). Dates read like "Tue 4 May 2027" (`formatCivilDate`), never ISO.
- **Delete flow** (Privacy → Delete account): explainer, typed confirm `DELETE`, fresh OTP or password proof, then the request; on success the app signs out and shows the archived notice. A trainer with active clients gets Z6-14's `409 active_clients` and the "end coaching first" copy with a link to Clients, and nothing is archived.
- **Reactivate flow:** after sign-in, an archived account goes to the reactivate screen instead of the app (the two `AuthProvider.tsx` files only route an archived account to the new screen; the screens live in the new files). "Reactivate my account" calls `POST /account/reactivation` and continues into the app; "Sign out" signs out. Coaching that ended with the deletion stays ended (D-W6-8), and the screen says so.
- Planned frame (not in the repo yet; FitBud UI draws it, Firoz reviews it): `BP1-delete-account-ios.png` (393x852), `BP1-delete-account-web.png` (1440x900), `BP1-reactivate-account-ios.png` (393x852) and `BP1-reactivate-account-web.png` (1440x900). Until the PNG is in `docs/design/mockups/png/`, the parts that need it stop and report; the rest runs.
