## Outcome
Two screens match their approved frames: a section's **See all** page hides the bottom tab bar (it is a full screen with Back, as drawn), and the Resolve sheet's outcome choices are tall radio rows instead of small chips.

## Context
- Z14-17 built the Attention tab and its sections; the W14 Report (Z14-17 and Z14-23 notes) lists two deviations from frames TM-01a6 and RP-03 for later.
- `AttentionTab.tsx:~880` renders `SectionAllScreen` inline inside the Attention tab, so `TrainerShell`'s tab bar (`TrainerShell.tsx:~619`) stays visible under it. TM-01a6 shows no tab bar.
- `ResolveSheet.tsx:~99-130` lays the outcome out as `Chip`s inside an `accessibilityRole="radiogroup"`; RP-03 draws one card of radio rows with dividers, each at least 44 pt tall, with a selected dot.
- Back behaviour is already tested (`TrainerShell.back-layers.test.tsx`, Z14-25); keep it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] While a section's See all page is open, the tab bar is **not rendered** (not just covered), the screen fills to the bottom safe area, the iOS back control reads "‹ Attention" (Android: back arrow and system back), and closing it restores the tab bar, the section, the scroll position and the filters. Recommended approach: Attention reports "full-screen child open" to `TrainerShell` through a prop callback and the shell hides the bar; if the overlay stack already models this, use it instead and say so.
- [ ] Other overlays and tabs keep the bar exactly as today (a test asserts each of Clients, Check-ins and Attention show it with no See all open).
- [ ] System/hardware Back and the in-screen Back from See all return to Attention with state kept; they never leave the app and never skip a layer (extend the Z14-25 tests).
- [ ] Resolve sheet: outcome choices are **radio rows** in one card with dividers (RP-03): each row at least 44 pt tall across the whole width, `accessibilityRole="radio"` with `accessibilityState.checked`, inside a `radiogroup`; one selected at a time with a clear selected dot; keyboard/screen-reader order top to bottom; light and dark. The Confirm button keeps `testID="resolve-confirm"`; rows get stable ids `resolve-outcome-<key>` (keys as in `resolveRecovery.ts`/the outcome list). Labels, order and helper text are unchanged. The outcome stays optional, Resolve is never disabled, and no outcome is pre-selected (D-W14-43).
- [ ] Small screen (360×640) and 120% text: the sheet scrolls rather than clipping; Confirm stays reachable above the home indicator.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` pass.
