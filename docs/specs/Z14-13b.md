## Outcome
A trainee who logged a meal wrongly can fix it themselves for 24 hours: change what they ate, the amounts, kcal, note or photo, or remove the log back to not logged, online or offline, and the coach sees that it was edited. After 24 hours the entry is read-only with a short explanation and a way to ask the coach.

## Context
- **D-W14-22** (Firoz, 24 Nov 2026; details decided by FitBud UI), D-W14-23 (coach marker, built in Z14-16), D-W14-19 (photo), D-W14-9 (offline is neutral), D-W14-3 (copy). Split from Z14-13 on 24 Nov 2026 so Z14-13 stays L; this runs after it in the same lane.
- API from Z14-11: `PATCH /meals/logs/:id`, `DELETE /meals/logs/:id`, `POST /meals/logs/:id/photo`, the `editableUntil` and `canRemove` fields, their `sync/push` mutation types and the `MEAL_EDIT_WINDOW_CLOSED` / `MEAL_EDITED_BY_COACH` / `MEAL_REMOVE_NOT_ALLOWED` codes. The app never computes the 24 h rule: it shows Edit, Remove and Add photo from `editableUntil` / `canRemove` (hide them once `editableUntil` passes while the screen is open).
- **Gate:** start only when D-W14-14 reads "Approved by Firoz, <date>" in `W14.md` (it does from 24 Nov 2026) and Z14-13 and Z14-11 have merged.

## Gap list
- A logged meal can't be changed by the trainee (the API refuses a second write with `COMPLIANCE_ALREADY_RECORDED`); the v3 TR-07g "Edit foods / Delete this log" sheet has no backend.
- Written against `main` at `2c9819d3`. Re-check each item on the latest `main` before building; drop what is done and say so in the PR (AGENTS.md).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Just-logged card (TR-07m4): small **Add photo** and **Edit** (44 pt) with "Editable until tomorrow, 5:20 pm"; shown on the most recently logged meal of the day until another meal is logged; every other logged card stays compact and opens the logged meal sheet on tap.
- [ ] Logged meal sheet (TR-07n): foods and kcal, the hint "Editable until {today|tomorrow|Sun 15 Nov}, {time} · Rahul sees any change", **Edit**, **Add photo**, **Remove this log** (Remove only when `canRemove`).
- [ ] Edit on an "Eaten as planned" entry opens TR-07n2 "Change this log": Changed the portion / Log it myself / Ate out, plus Remove this log. Each path opens its screen prefilled from the plan and saves as an edit, not a new log.
- [ ] Edit on a changed-portion entry opens TR-07n4 "Edit portion" prefilled with the logged amounts, Reset to plan, Remove this log, "Save changes" top right and at the bottom; a food-search entry opens food search with its items; an ate-out entry opens TR-07n5 "Edit ate out" with kcal and note prefilled and "Was 1,120 kcal".
- [ ] Remove: confirm per TR-07n3 ("Remove the {meal} log?" … "Rahul sees that you removed it."), then the meal is not logged and can be logged again.
- [ ] Saved (TR-07n6): toast "{Meal} updated · Rahul sees the change", totals and ring update at once (optimistic, from the outbox), and the card shows a small neutral **Edited** tag (from `lastEditedByRole`).
- [ ] Locked (TR-07n7): when `editableUntil` is null or past, no Edit / Remove / Add photo; a neutral note with a lock icon "Logged entries can be changed for 24 hours." and "Ask Rahul to fix it" (wa.me prefill, never auto-sent). Coach-logged or coach-edited entries are locked the same way.
- [ ] Offline (TR-07n8): edit screens show the neutral banner "You're offline. Your change saves on your phone and sends when you're back."; edits and removals queue as outbox mutations with `recordVersion` and paint at once; a server refusal (window passed, coach edited it, conflict) reverts to the server version and shows a friendly line in sync status ("Couldn't change lunch: the 24 hours had passed. Ask Rahul to fix it."), with conflicts using the existing TR-19b flow.
- [ ] Copy follows D-W14-3 (no codes); dates and times read like "Sun 15 Nov, 5:20 pm"; `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` pass.
