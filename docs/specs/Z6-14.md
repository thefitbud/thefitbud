## Outcome
A trainee or trainer can delete their account from the app or the web; the account is archived for 6 months and can be reactivated by signing in again, then it is purged. Permanent deletion straight away is by email to tech@thefitbud.com, handled by support with a runbook. Meets Apple 5.1.1(v) and Google Play's account-deletion rule.

## Context
- **Split 4 Nov 2026 (PLAN-v2 D-P2-6):** this spec keeps archive, status, reactivation, the archived-actor middleware and the public page. The **Purge** and **Permanent deletion now** bullets below are built by **Z6-17** (`account-purge.ts`, its test, `docs/runbooks/account-deletion.md`); read them here for context only.
- Covers master-list (v13) items BP1 (API part). Everything the implementer needs from those rows is pasted here.
- D-W6-7 (Firoz, 3 Nov) and D-W6-8 (FitBud UI, 3 Nov) in `docs/specs/batches/W6.md`. **Now beta scope** (Firoz, 4 Nov 2026: account deletion is part of the beta, PLAN-v2 D-P2-3). Uses Z6-16's `archived` state, `archived_at` and `purge_after`.
- **Delete (archive):** `POST /account/deletion` (authenticated; needs a fresh OTP or password proof within 5 minutes). A trainer with active clients is blocked with `409 active_clients` ("End coaching with your clients first."). Otherwise in one transaction: `account_state = 'archived'`, `archived_at = now`, `purge_after = now + 6 calendar months`; end every active relationship through the existing lifecycle (exceptions close with `coaching_ended`); delete device tokens and sessions (signs out every device). Data is kept untouched while archived. The Firebase user is kept so the same sign-in works for reactivation. Response carries `purgeAfter`.
- **While archived:** the auth middleware lets an archived actor reach only `GET /account/status` and `POST /account/reactivation`; every other route returns `403 account_archived` with `purgeAfter`. Archived users never appear in trainer pickers, invites or search; their ended relationships still read as ended for the other party.
- **Reactivate:** signing in again during the 6 months succeeds and returns `accountState: "archived"` and `purgeAfter`; the app shows the reactivate screen (Z12-29). `POST /account/reactivation` sets `account_state = 'active'` and clears both dates. Coaching relationships stay ended: a trainer or trainee restarts coaching through the existing reactivation and invite flows (no automatic restart). Reactivating after `purge_after` is impossible (the account is gone).
- **Purge:** `runAccountPurge(env, now)` in `apps/api/src/jobs/account-purge.ts` (stub from Z5-4, called from the existing cron) picks archived users with `purge_after <= now`, a few per run, and for each: deletes media in R2, identities and remaining tokens; anonymises rows other users' records need (trainer notes and check-in reviews keep the trainer side; the person's name becomes "Deleted user"); removes the Firebase user via Admin REST (secret already used by push, no new secret); finally deletes or anonymises the users row. Idempotent and resumable after a crash.
- **Permanent deletion now:** the user emails tech@thefitbud.com from the address or phone on the account. Support verifies the sender matches the account, then follows `docs/runbooks/account-deletion.md`: archive the account if it is not archived, set `purge_after` to now with one `wrangler d1 execute <db> --remote` statement (by user id, no personal data in the command history), and confirm by reply once the next purge run reports it. No admin endpoint and no new secret. The implementer writes the runbook; support (FitBud UI on the box) runs it.
- **Public page** for the Play listing: `GET /account/delete` (HTML, no data, `account-deletion-page.ts`) says: "Delete your account in the FitBud app: More → Privacy → Delete account. Your account is archived for 6 months and you can bring it back by signing in again. After 6 months it is deleted for good. To delete it permanently straight away, email tech@thefitbud.com from the email or phone number on your account."

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Delete, status, reactivate and purge paths as above, each tested; purge is idempotent and resumable after a crash mid-way.
- [ ] An archived user can sign in, sees `archived` with `purgeAfter`, can reactivate, and is blocked from every other route until then.
- [ ] After purge, sign-in for that identity fails, no row holds the user's email, phone or name, and the other party's history still reads correctly.
- [ ] A trainer with active clients is blocked with `409 active_clients` (D-W6-7).
- [ ] Setting `purge_after` to now (runbook step) makes the next purge run delete the account; the runbook is in `docs/runbooks/account-deletion.md`.
- [ ] No personal data in logs.
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` pass.
