- **D-W14-4 External meeting links only** on calls (Zoom, Meet etc., `https://` only, opened outside the app); no in-app video or voice calling. Carried over (v12).
- **D-W14-5 Online-only coaches get calls only:** read from the coaching mode on the subscription (W6 Z6-3/Z6-8); the API rejects in-person sessions for them. Carried over (v13 S1b, S3b, S6c).
- **D-W14-6 Reminders go to the trainer only:** push 45 minutes before a session and 15 minutes before a call (v13 S1d), through the existing Expo push path, respecting notification settings; trainee reminders are not in this batch. Decided by FitBud UI, 3 Nov (frame 205 is the trainer's reminder; push shipped in the beta, so v13's "needs X4" is met).
- **D-W14-7 Coach-logged sets:** one or many present clients, same exercise, with Undo for 10 minutes; trainees cannot edit them but can suggest a change that the coach accepts or declines. Carried over (v13 S2a, S2b, S7); the 10-minute Undo window decided by FitBud UI, 3 Nov.
- **D-W14-8 Calendar:** Sessions is its own nav item on web; the week runs Monday–Sunday, 6 am–9 pm; calls dashed teal, sessions solid. Carried over (v13 S3a).
- **D-W14-9 Session logging on web and iPad uses new DS6 frames** (D15, locked 23 Oct); FitBud UI draws them and Firoz reviews them. IP-03, IP-04 and IP-06 are reference only. Firoz, 3 Nov.
