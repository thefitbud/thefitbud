## Outcome
Every Maestro flow that reaches the trainer's Remind sheet and the review panel uses words and ids the app has today, so **F20 passes** and no flow waits for a button that was renamed or removed. A test fails the build the next time a flow names a `testID` that no longer exists.

## Context
- The W14 Report (coordinator summary, 25 Nov 2026) lists five flows made stale by Z14-15 (Nudge becomes Remind) and Z14-23 (no ⋯ menu on the review panel). F20 calls `open-sheet.yaml`, so F20 fails until it is fixed; Z14-3, Z14-15, Z14-22 and Z14-23 all point at F20 in their device-run commands.
- **D-W14-33** (this batch), **D-W14-38** (selector guard). The old open PR #521 ("Z12-61: v3 Maestro flow selectors", 21 Nov, conflicting) is **not** the fix and must not be merged or reused; it predates Z14-23.
- File names that say "nudge" (`F20-triage-nudge.yaml`, `nudge-client.yaml`) are referenced by the Report commands, `device.yml` globs and `docs/qa/W12/maestro-map.md`: **do not rename any file**. Internal ids such as `client-nudge`, `nudge-send` and the API's `activity_nudge` stay (D-W14-15 changed the words, not the ids).
